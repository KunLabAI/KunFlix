## 一、流行音乐（Pop）完整模板

### 标准配置
- **BPM**: 110-128
- **调性**: C Major / G Major / Ab Major
- **核心编制**: Drum Machine (punchy kick, crisp snare, shimmering hi-hats) + Synth Bass + Electric Guitar (clean/light crunch) + Synth Pad + Piano
- **效果处理**: 适量混响、侧链压缩、人声自动调音（slight pitch correction）

### 完整提示词
```
A modern pop song in G major at 118 BPM. Punchy electronic drums with crisp snare and shimmering hi-hats, warm synth bass, bright synth leads, clean electric guitar arpeggios, and lush vocal harmonies. Uplifting, feel-good summer anthem mood. Female soprano vocals with catchy melodic hook.

[Verse 1]
Sunrise painting colors on my wall
I can feel the world is gonna call
Every moment feels like something new
All I need is here with you

[Pre-Chorus]
Can you feel it rising in the air

[Chorus]
This is our time, this is our song
We've been waiting oh so long
Light it up and let it shine
Everything is yours and mine

[Verse 2]
Dancing through the city streets at night
Every star is burning ever bright

[Chorus]
This is our time, this is our song
We've been waiting oh so long

[Outro]
Our time... our song... (fade)
```

## 二、电影配乐——史诗管弦（Epic Orchestral）完整模板

### 标准配置
- **BPM**: 130-150（战斗）/ 60-80（抒情）
- **调性**: D Minor / C Minor / E Minor
- **核心编制**: Full String Section (Violin I & II, Viola, Cello, Double Bass) + Brass (Horns, Trumpets, Trombones, Tuba) + Timpani + Taiko + Snare + Crash Cymbals + Choir
- **效果处理**: 大厅混响、宽广立体声、动态压缩

### 完整提示词——战斗场景
```
Epic orchestral battle score in D minor at 144 BPM. Thundering timpani and taiko drums drive relentless rhythm, aggressive string ostinato (16th notes) builds tension, powerful French horn melody rises above the chaos, trumpet fanfares punctuate climactic moments, massive choir chants in Latin. Dynamic arc: starts with ominous low strings, builds through layered percussion, explodes into full orchestra fortissimo. Cinematic, heroic, intense. Instrumental only.

Timeline:
[0:00-0:20] Ominous intro — solo cello tremolo, distant timpani rolls
[0:20-0:50] Building tension — string ostinato enters, percussion layers build
[0:50-1:20] First battle — full brass melody, driving rhythm, choir enters
[1:20-1:40] Brief respite — solo French horn over sustained strings
[1:40-2:10] Final assault — maximum intensity, all sections fortissimo
[2:10-2:30] Victory theme — major key resolution, triumphant brass
```

### 完整提示词——情感抒情
```
Neoclassical film score in A minor at 68 BPM. Solo piano with gentle sustain pedal playing arpeggiated chords, gradually joined by intimate string quartet (two violins, viola, cello). Melancholic yet beautiful, like watching snow fall through a window. Subtle crescendo in the middle section before returning to quiet resolution. Instrumental only, no vocals.
```

## 三、电子音乐（EDM/House）完整模板

### 标准配置
- **BPM**: 124-128（House）/ 140-150（Trance）/ 160-175（DnB）
- **调性**: A Minor / F Minor / D Minor
- **核心编制**: 4-on-the-floor Kick + Layered Hi-hats + Clap/Snare + Sub Bass (side-chained) + Supersaw Lead + Filter Sweep FX + Vocal Chops + Atmospheric Pads
- **效果处理**: 重侧链压缩、DJ滤波器扫频、Reverb Tails、Delay Throws

### 完整提示词——Progressive House
```
Progressive house track at 126 BPM in F minor. Deep, warm kick drum with tight side-chain compression pumping the mix, rolling hi-hat patterns, ethereal female vocal chops processed with heavy reverb, massive supersaw synth chords in the drop, evolving filter sweeps during builds, and a deep sub-bass providing low-end weight. Euphoric, driving, festival anthem energy. Instrumental with vocal chops only.

Timeline:
[0:00-0:30] Intro — atmospheric pads, filtered percussion enters gradually
[0:30-1:00] Build-up — snare roll crescendo, rising white noise sweep, synth melody teased
[1:00-1:30] Drop — full supersaw chords, driving bass, vocal chops, maximum energy
[1:30-2:00] Breakdown — strip to pads and piano, emotional melody
[2:00-2:20] Build-up 2 — more intense, added percussion layers
[2:20-2:50] Drop 2 — variation with added arpeggiated synth layer
[2:50-3:10] Outro — energy gradually decreasing, final reverb tail
```

- **BPM**: 70-90

```
Lo-fi hip hop chill beat at 78 BPM in Eb major. Mellow Fender Rhodes chords with warm chorus effect, dusty boom-bap drums processed through tape emulation, gentle vinyl crackle throughout, smooth jazz guitar sample with rolled-off highs, deep warm sub bass, and occasional rain ambience in the background. Cozy, nostalgic, late-night study session vibe. Instrumental only, no vocals.

Avoid: harsh sounds, distortion, aggressive drums, bright synths.
```

## 五、中国风/东方传统 完整模板

### 标准配置
- **BPM**: 60-100
- **调性**: 宫调式（Pentatonic）/ A Minor Pentatonic / D Minor Pentatonic
- **核心编制**: 古筝（Guzheng）+ 竹笛（Dizi）+ 琵琶（Pipa）+ 二胡（Erhu）+ 扬琴（Yangqin）+ 轻柔打击（木鱼、碰铃）
- **效果处理**: 自然混响模拟古典厅堂、轻微立体声扩展

### 完整提示词——山水意境
```
Traditional Chinese instrumental piece at 76 BPM in A minor pentatonic scale. Guzheng (古筝) plays flowing arpeggiated patterns as the lead, dizi (竹笛) provides a haunting counter-melody with gentle vibrato, yangqin (扬琴) adds shimmering harmonic support, and soft wooden percussion marks subtle rhythmic accents. Evokes misty mountain landscapes at dawn, serene flowing rivers, and ancient poetry. Ethereal, poetic, meditative. Instrumental only.

Avoid: western drums, electric instruments, modern synths, fast tempo.
```

### 完整提示词——武侠风格
```
Chinese wuxia (martial arts) cinematic music at 110 BPM in D minor pentatonic. Dramatic erhu (二胡) solo with intense vibrato as lead, pipa (琵琶) rapid tremolo picking for action moments, orchestral strings for depth, Chinese drums (堂鼓) and cymbals for rhythmic intensity, and dizi flute for melodic contrast in quieter passages. Heroic, dramatic, flowing between calm contemplation and fierce combat energy. Instrumental only.
```

## 六、爵士乐 完整模板

### 标准配置
- **BPM**: 120-160（Swing）/ 60-80（Ballad）/ 100-130（Bossa）
- **调性**: Bb Major / F Major / Eb Major（爵士常用降号调）
- **核心编制**: Acoustic Piano（voicings: 7th/9th/13th chords）+ Upright Bass（walking line）+ Drums（brush/ride）+ Optional: Trumpet / Tenor Sax / Guitar
- **效果处理**: 自然厅堂混响、温暖均衡、最少处理

### 完整提示词——Swing Jazz
```
Classic swing jazz trio in Bb major at 140 BPM with shuffle/swing feel. Acoustic grand piano playing rich jazz voicings with 7th and 9th chords, upright acoustic bass walking quarter-note lines, and drums with ride cymbal swing pattern and brush snare. Sophisticated, warm, smoky jazz club atmosphere in the 1960s. Add a tenor saxophone solo in the middle section with bebop-inspired improvisation. Instrumental only.
```

### 完整提示词——Bossa Nova
```
Bossa nova piece in F major at 108 BPM. Nylon-string classical guitar playing characteristic bossa nova rhythm pattern (syncopated chord comping), soft brushed percussion with shaker, gentle upright bass, and warm piano adding subtle harmonic color. Romantic, breezy, Rio de Janeiro sunset atmosphere. Instrumental only.

Avoid: drums with heavy backbeat, electric instruments, distortion.
```

## 八、氛围/环境音乐 完整模板

### 标准配置
- **BPM**: 自由节拍 或 55-70
- **调性**: C Major / F Major / 无明确调性（Atonal Textures）
- **核心编制**: Evolving Synth Pads + Granular Synthesis Textures + Sine Wave Sub Drones + Reversed Piano/Guitar + Field Recordings (water, wind, birds) + Crystal Bells/Chimes + Subtle filtered noise
- **效果处理**: 极长混响（6-12秒）、大量延迟、颗粒合成、空间化处理

### 完整提示词
```
Ambient meditation soundscape in C major with free-flowing tempo around 60 BPM. Slowly evolving granular synthesis textures, deep warm sine wave drone providing a stable tonal center, distant crystalline bell tones with 10-second reverb tails, reversed piano notes floating in the stereo field, gentle field recording of a mountain stream underneath, and subtle breath-like filtered white noise. Deeply peaceful, expansive, transcendent. No clear rhythm or beat. Instrumental only.

Avoid: drums, percussion, bass drops, sudden changes, vocals, distortion.
```

## 十、负向提示（Avoid）常用参考

| 要排除的元素 | 提示词写法 |
|------------|-----------|
| 不要人声 | Avoid: vocals, singing, voice, choir |
| 不要打击乐 | Avoid: drums, percussion, rhythm section |
| 不要电子元素 | Avoid: synths, electronic sounds, drum machine |
| 不要失真 | Avoid: distortion, overdrive, harsh sounds |
| 不要快节奏 | Avoid: fast tempo, energetic rhythm, double-time |
| 排除特定乐器 | Avoid: guitar, piano, brass (直接列出) |
| 不要突然变化 | Avoid: sudden changes, aggressive dynamics |
